# Evaluation Metrics

Task 8 summarizes metrics generated by the offline Reliability Guardrails.
These metrics are deterministic heuristics for sample demonstrations, not
legal, financial, or procurement judgments.

| Metric | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Citation coverage | Linked claims divided by total claims. |
| Temporal leakage rate | Claims linked to future or not-yet-effective evidence. |
| Heuristic grounded claim rate | Claims grounded by lexical/keyphrase overlap. |
| Unsupported claim rate | Claims classified as unsupported or with no evidence. |
| Policy compliance rate | Policy rules passed divided by policy checks considered. |
| Calculation traceability | Numeric claims whose numbers can be traced in linked snippets or text. |
| Consistency warning rate | Consistency warnings divided by relevant claims when available. |
| Overall score | Weighted aggregate from the guardrail pipeline configuration. |
| Route counts | Counts of `final_report`, `retry`, `human_review`, and `stop` workflow routes. |

## Interpretation

A high score means the offline checks found fewer deterministic issues. It does
not mean a recommendation is correct.

Heuristic groundedness is not semantic entailment.
