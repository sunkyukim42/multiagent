# Synthetic Procurement Policy Sample

This policy is synthetic, illustrative, and non-confidential. It is intended only for offline RAG tests.

## 1. Approval Thresholds

Purchases above the standard category threshold require manager approval. Strategic supplier commitments require documented review of price volatility, lead time exposure, and supplier reliability.

## 2. Hedge And Renegotiation Escalation

When input costs are oil-sensitive or freight-sensitive, category owners may recommend BUY_EARLY, HEDGE, or RENEGOTIATE. Escalation is required when volatility could affect quarterly budget commitments.

## 3. Supplier Switching

Supplier switching may be considered when reliability issues, compliance risk, or contract terms create unacceptable exposure. SWITCH_SUPPLIER recommendations require a transition plan and risk owner approval.
