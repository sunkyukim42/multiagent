# Synthetic Supplier Risk Note

This document is synthetic, illustrative, and non-confidential. It is intended only for offline RAG tests.

Supplier reliability should be reviewed alongside lead time risk, compliance risk, price volatility, and contract terms. A supplier with repeated delivery misses may require escalation even if current pricing is favorable.

For oil-sensitive inputs, price volatility can justify BUY_EARLY or HEDGE actions when inventory policy allows. If contract language permits adjustment, RENEGOTIATE may reduce exposure without requiring an immediate supplier change.

Recommended review factors: reliability history, compliance status, lead time trend, switching cost, and budget impact.
